A funding gap that bursaries don’t cover

For schools

For asset-rich, cash-constrained families, means-tested bursaries aren't an option due to the value of their homes, so bursars are often left with no way to help. Selina gives these families a genuine route to fund fees, giving your school greater confidence in fee collection.

For families

Rising fees, siblings at school, or a change in income can turn every term into a fresh financial scramble, even for families who can genuinely afford the fees over time.
